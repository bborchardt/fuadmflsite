Save MFL pages

On any page of the league on MFL, the bookmark reads every page v2 reads, draws or restyles, as MFL serves it to whoever is logged in, and saves them as one zip. It takes about a minute and only reads pages. Emails, phone numbers, owners' names and MFL's apiKey are taken out.
