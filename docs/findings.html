Week 4 Site Check

The league site works on desktop and phones. The problems are in the experience: a Contracts tab members have to dig through, a dated look, and a deploy process that drifts. Each line below is the whole point.

Six problems, three pieces of work

    The Contracts tab is a reference document, not a tool

Ruled out

Considered and set aside

  • A separate companion site (Option 3). A second destination is a new navigation layer, and it can't tell who's viewing.
  • A proxy (Option 4). Against MFL's API terms.
  • Leaving MFL (Option 6). Would lose the custom rules MFL's openness allows.
  • A public league-history site (Option 5). Optional, and only after A–C.
Ground rules

From your decisions

  • The site must work well in a phone browser.
  • No new navigation layer. Everything lives inside a tab.
  • The custom tabs stay as members know them.
  • Stay within MFL's rules, and keep upkeep modest.
